# Fundamental rights impact assessment (Article 27)

## Who must perform a FRIA

Applies to high-risk AI systems as defined in **Article 6(2)** (Annex III), **except** systems intended to be used in the area listed in **Annex III point 2** (critical infrastructure).

| Addressee | In scope? |
|-----------|-----------|
| Deployers that are **bodies governed by public law** | Yes |
| Deployers that are **private entities providing public services** | Yes |
| **All deployers** of Annex III point **5(b)** (creditworthiness / credit scoring of natural persons) | Yes |
| **All deployers** of Annex III point **5(c)** (risk assessment and pricing in life and health insurance) | Yes |
| Deployers of Annex III **point 2** (critical infrastructure) | **No** — excluded by Art. 27(1) |
| Other private deployers of other Annex III systems who are not providing public services | **No** — Art. 27 not triggered |

Timing: perform the assessment **prior to deploying** the system. The obligation in Art. 27(1) applies to the **first use**. In similar cases the deployer may rely on previously conducted FRIAs or existing impact assessments carried out by that deployer (Art. 27(2)). Similar cases exist where the relevant deployer already completed a FRIA for a similar high-risk AI system used in comparable contexts, and has already assessed the similar risks.

## Content — Article 27(1)(a)–(f)

| Point | Required content |
|-------|------------------|
| (a) | A description of the deployer's **processes** in which the high-risk AI system will be used, in line with its intended purpose |
| (b) | A description of the **period of time** within which, and the **frequency** with which, each high-risk AI system is intended to be used |
| (c) | The **categories of natural persons and groups** likely to be affected by its use in the specific context |
| (d) | The **specific risks of harm** likely to have an impact on the **fundamental rights** of those persons or groups, taking into account the information given by the provider pursuant to Art. 13 |
| (e) | A description of the implementation of **human oversight** measures, according to the instructions for use |
| (f) | The **measures** to be taken in the case of the materialisation of those risks, including internal governance arrangements and **complaint mechanisms** |

## Notification — Article 27(3)

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| What | Notify the **market surveillance authority** of the **results** of the assessment |
| How | Submit the filled-out **template** referred to in Art. 27(5) (drawn up by the Commission via implementing act) |
| What it is not | Not a generic “regulatory filing,” licence application, or CE-marking dossier. It is MSA notification of FRIA results using the Commission template |

## Relationship with DPIA — Article 27(4)

| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| GDPR | If any Art. 27 obligation is already met through a data protection impact assessment under **Article 35 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679**, the FRIA shall be conducted **in conjunction with** that DPIA |
| Law Enforcement Directive | Same rule where the equivalent assessment is under **Article 27 of Directive (EU) 2016/680** |
| Effect | Do not duplicate covered elements; do not skip FRIA elements that the DPIA does not address (including period/frequency of use, Art. 27(1)(b)) |
