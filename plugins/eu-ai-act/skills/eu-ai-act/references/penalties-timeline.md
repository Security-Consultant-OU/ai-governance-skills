# EU AI Act penalties and application dates

## Application dates

Status is as of **25 August 2026**. Use **In force / Upcoming**. Do not describe past application dates as “before [date]” remaining deadlines.

| Application date | Status | What applies | Who is affected | Key reference |
|------------------|--------|--------------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 August 2024 | In force | Regulation published and entered into force | All entities | Art. 113 |
| 2 February 2025 | In force | Article 5 prohibitions; AI literacy (Article 4) | Providers and deployers | Arts. 5, 4 |
| 2 August 2025 | In force | Chapter V GPAI model obligations; governance; codes of practice; notified-body designation rules | GPAI model providers; notified bodies; Member States | Chapter V, Art. 56 |
| 2 August 2026 | In force | High-risk requirements (Arts. 8–15); conformity assessment; CE marking; registration; deployer duties (Arts. 26–27); **Article 50 transparency** | High-risk providers and deployers; importers; distributors; Art. 50 addressees | Chapters III–IV; Art. 50 |
| 2 August 2027 | Upcoming | Obligations for high-risk AI systems that are safety components of (or themselves) products covered by Annex I (Art. 6(1) path) | Product manufacturers integrating AI into regulated products | Art. 6(1), Art. 113 |

Article 50 has applied since 2 August 2026 and is **already in force**.

## Penalty structure

| Violation type | Maximum fine (entity) | Maximum fine (SME/startup) | Article |
|---------------|----------------------|---------------------------|---------|
| Prohibited AI practices (Article 5) | 35,000,000 EUR or 7% of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher | Proportionately lower amounts | Art. 99(3) |
| Non-compliance with other obligations (including high-risk requirements and provider/deployer duties) | 15,000,000 EUR or 3% of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher | Proportionately lower amounts | Art. 99(4) |
| Supplying incorrect, incomplete, or misleading information to authorities | 7,500,000 EUR or 1% of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher | Proportionately lower amounts | Art. 99(5) |
| Non-compliance by EU institutions, bodies, offices, and agencies | Up to 1,500,000 EUR (prohibited practices), 750,000 EUR (other violations) | N/A | Art. 99(6) |

## Penalty considerations

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Proportionality | Penalties must be effective, proportionate, and dissuasive |
| SME/startup protections | Fines capped at proportionately lower amounts for SMEs and startups |
| Factors in determination | Nature, gravity, and duration of infringement; intentional or negligent character; actions taken to mitigate; size and market share; previous infringements; degree of cooperation with authorities; manner in which the infringement became known |
| Turnover calculation | Based on total worldwide annual turnover of the preceding financial year; for group entities, the turnover of the ultimate parent undertaking |
| Member State enforcement | Each Member State lays down rules on penalties applicable to infringements by operators; may include non-monetary penalties (warnings, compliance orders) |

## Enforcement bodies

| Body | Role | Scope |
|------|------|-------|
| AI Office (European Commission) | Oversight of GPAI models; development of codes of practice; monitoring systemic-risk models | EU-wide; GPAI-specific enforcement |
| National competent authorities | Market surveillance; enforcement of high-risk AI obligations; investigation and penalty imposition | Member State level; AI Act provisions other than GPAI model supervision |
| National market surveillance authorities | Monitoring AI systems on the market; for Annex III point 1 in LE/immigration/asylum or EU-institution use, may **act as notified body** (Art. 43(1), Art. 74(8)–(9)) | Member State level; market-placed AI systems |
| European AI Board | Coordination between Member States; consistency of application; advisory role | EU-wide; advisory and coordination |
| Notified bodies | Conformity assessment under Annex VII when that path applies (Annex III point 1 where chosen or mandatory) | Designated by Member States; Annex VII procedures |

## Compliance posture by application wave

| Wave | Status | Action still required if not done | Risk of non-compliance |
|------|--------|-----------------------------------|------------------------|
| Article 5 / Article 4 | In force since 2 February 2025 | Audit systems against the Article 5 list; cease prohibited practices; maintain AI literacy measures | Up to 35M EUR / 7% turnover for prohibited-practice violations |
| GPAI Chapter V | In force since 2 August 2025 | Annex XI documentation; copyright policy; public training-content summary; downstream information; Art. 52 notification within 2 weeks of the systemic-risk threshold | Up to 15M EUR / 3% turnover |
| High-risk + Article 50 | In force since 2 August 2026 | Conformity assessment; Annex IV documentation; QMS; EU database registration; CE marking; Art. 26 deployer duties; Art. 27 FRIA where gated; Art. 50 marking and notices | Up to 15M EUR / 3% turnover |
| Annex I Art. 6(1) path | Upcoming — 2 August 2027 | Integrate Arts. 9–15 into the sectoral conformity assessment (Art. 43(3)) | Up to 15M EUR / 3% turnover |
