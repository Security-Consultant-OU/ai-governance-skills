# Annex IV technical documentation — section checklist

Paraphrased checklist. Do not paste Annex IV verbatim. **Default document** when the user does not specify which artefact to generate.

| Annex IV section | What the documentation must cover | Typical evidence |
|------------------|-----------------------------------|------------------|
| 1. General description of the AI system | Intended purpose; provider name and the version of the system reflecting its relation to previous versions; how the system interacts with hardware or software that is not part of it; versions of relevant software or firmware; forms in which the system is placed on the market or put into service; hardware on which it is intended to run; where it is a component of products, photographs or illustrations of external features, marking and internal layout; user-interface provided to the deployer; instructions for use for the deployer and a basic description of that interface | Intended-purpose statement; version map; architecture overview; hardware/software bill; deployer UI description; instructions for use |
| 2. Detailed description of elements and of the development process | Methods and steps performed for the development, including where relevant the use of pre-trained systems or tools provided by third parties and how these were used, integrated or modified; design specifications (general logic of the system and of the algorithms; key design choices including rationale and assumptions; what the system is designed to optimise for and the relevance of different parameters; expected output and output quality; decisions about any trade-offs); architecture, indicating how software components build on or feed into each other and integrate into the overall processing; computational resources used to develop, train, test and validate; data requirements (datasheets describing training methodologies and techniques and the training datasets used, including information about their provenance, scope and main characteristics; how data were obtained and selected; labelling procedures; data-cleaning methodologies); assessment of the human-oversight measures needed in view of the intended purpose and of Art. 14; pre-determined changes to the system and its performance; validation and testing procedures used, including information about the validation and testing data used and their main characteristics; metrics used to measure accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity and compliance with other relevant requirements; test logs and test reports dated and signed by the responsible persons | Design spec; model card; data cards; third-party component register; V&V plan and signed test reports; human-oversight design note; change-control log |
| 3. Monitoring, functioning and control | Capacities and limitations in view of the intended purpose; accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity metrics; foreseeable unintended outcomes and sources of risks to health, safety and fundamental rights; human-oversight measures needed, including technical measures to facilitate interpretation of outputs; input-data specifications | Operating limits; residual-risk statement; oversight specification; input-data spec |
| 4. Appropriateness of performance metrics | A detailed description of the appropriateness of the performance metrics for the specific AI system | Metric-selection rationale; known metric limitations |
| 5. Risk management system | A detailed description of the risk-management system in accordance with Art. 9 | Risk-management plan; risk register; residual-risk record |
| 6. Relevant changes | A list of relevant changes made by the provider to the system through its lifecycle | Change log; version history linked to assessments |
| 7. Standards and other specifications | A list of the harmonised standards applied in full or in part; where harmonised standards have not been applied or have been applied only in part, a detailed description of the solutions adopted to meet the requirements of Chapter III Section 2, including a list of other relevant standards or common specifications applied | Standards matrix; gap analysis where Art. 40/41 instruments are partial |
| 8. EU declaration of conformity | A copy of the EU declaration of conformity | Signed Art. 47 / Annex V declaration |
| 9. Post-market performance evaluation | A detailed description of the system in place to evaluate the AI system performance in the post-market phase in accordance with Art. 72, including the post-market monitoring plan | PMM plan; data-collection procedures; feedback loop into Art. 9 |

SME providers may provide the information in a single simplified form covering the same substance (Annex IV last paragraph). Keep documentation up to date for the lifetime of the system plus 10 years after it is placed on the market or put into service (Art. 18).
