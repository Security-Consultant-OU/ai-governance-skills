# Enforcement, institutions, and grace period

**Status (25 August 2026):** The Act is **in force** as of **22 January 2026**. Enforcement Decree No. 36053 is in force. MSIT grace on most investigations and fines runs through **at least January 2027**, except serious harm. Do not state that decrees have not been issued.

## Instruments

| Instrument | Citation | Status |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Framework Act on the Development of Artificial Intelligence and the Creation of a Foundation for Trust (인공지능 기본법) | Act No. 20676 of 21 January 2025, amended Act No. 21311 of 20 January 2026 | In force 22 January 2026 |
| Enforcement Decree | Presidential Decree No. 36053 | In force |

## Institutions

| Body | Role | Article / function |
|------|------|--------------------|
| Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) | Competent ministry for Arts. 31–36 implementation and Art. 43 fines | Competent authority |
| Presidential Council on National AI Strategy | National AI strategy and policy coordination | Under the **President** (not the Prime Minister) |
| KOSA | Help desk for operators | Implementation support |
| NIA and KOSA | Guidelines for Arts. 31–36 practice | Guidance |
| KISA | AI **security** guidance | Not the main Art. 31–36 implementer |

## Administrative fines (Art. 43)

| Breach | Article | Maximum |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Failure of Art. 31 notice | Art. 31, Art. 43 | **KRW 30 million** |
| Failure to designate a domestic representative | Art. 36, Art. 43 | **KRW 30 million** |
| Corrective-order or inspection failures | Art. 43 | **KRW 30 million** |
| Fines “to be defined by later decree” | — | **Incorrect** — Art. 43 is in force |

Art. 43 is not an EU-style 7% global-turnover fine.

## MSIT grace period

| Topic | Source | Rule |
|-------|--------|------|
| Duration | MSIT grace (Act in force 22 Jan 2026) | At least **one year** from entry into force — through **at least January 2027** |
| What is restrained | MSIT grace | Most **investigations and administrative fines** |
| Exception | MSIT grace | **Serious harm** — grace does not apply |
| What is not delayed | Act No. 20676 / 21311; Decree No. 36053 | The Act itself — duties exist from 22 January 2026 |
| Operator posture | Arts. 31–36, Art. 43 | Implement now; do not wait for the grace window to close |

## Comparison (enforcement design)

| Korea | Instrument | Closest analogue | Do not equate |
|-------|------------|------------------|---------------|
| In force 22 Jan 2026 + grace on most fines | Act + MSIT grace | EU **phased application** calendar | Korea is not “not yet applicable” |
| Art. 43 ≤ KRW 30 million | Art. 43 | EU up to 7% turnover / EUR 35 million | Different scale and legal design |
| Optional Art. 33 confirmation | Art. 33 | EU conformity assessment / CE | Art. 33 is optional status confirmation, not a licence |
