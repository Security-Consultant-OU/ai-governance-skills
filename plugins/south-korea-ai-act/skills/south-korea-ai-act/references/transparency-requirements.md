# Art. 31 transparency (by paragraph)

Art. 31 is **not** a duty for all AI systems. Split every review by paragraph. Failing Art. 31 **notice** can attract an administrative fine up to **KRW 30 million** (Art. 43).

## Art. 31(1) — prior notice that the product or service uses AI

| Item | Article | Rule |
|------|---------|------|
| Who is told | Art. 31(1) | Persons provided with the product or service (Art. 2(8) users) |
| What | Art. 31(1) | Prior notice that the product/service **uses AI** |
| When it applies | Art. 31(1) | **High-impact (고영향 AI)** or **generative** only |
| When it does **not** apply | Art. 31(1) | Ordinary AI that is neither high-impact nor generative |
| Timing | Art. 31(1) | **Prior** to provision of the product/service |
| Fine | Art. 43 | Up to KRW 30 million for failing Art. 31 notice |
| Language / clarity | Art. 31(1) | Understandable to the person provided with the product (Korean for Korea-facing services) |

| System | Art. 31(1) notice? | Why |
|--------|--------------------|-----|
| High-impact hiring ranker | Yes | High-impact |
| Generative chatbot | Yes | Generative |
| Internal spreadsheet macro with no gen / not high-impact | No | Outside Art. 31(1) |
| Recommendation widget, not high-impact, not generative | No | Outside Art. 31(1) |

## Art. 31(2) — label generative outputs

| Item | Article | Rule |
|------|---------|------|
| What | Art. 31(2) | Label outputs as generated by AI |
| Trigger | Art. 31(2) | Generative outputs |
| Who implements | Art. 2(7), Art. 31(2) | Development operator designs the label; use/service operator presents it |
| Not required under this paragraph | Art. 31(2) | Non-generative high-impact systems (those may still need Art. 31(1) notice) |

## Art. 31(3) — extra notice for hard-to-distinguish synthetic audio, image, or video

| Item | Article | Rule |
|------|---------|------|
| What | Art. 31(3) | Extra notice that the content is synthetic |
| Trigger | Art. 31(3) | Synthetic **audio, image, or video** that is **hard to distinguish from real** |
| Exception | Art. 31(3) | **Artistic / creative** uses |
| Relationship to 31(2) | Art. 31(2)–(3) | 31(2) labels generative outputs generally; 31(3) adds notice where realism can deceive |
| Not this paragraph | Art. 31(3) | Ordinary labelled text generation that is not A/V/I, or obviously synthetic media |

## Art. 3(2) and Art. 34(1)2 — do not fold into Art. 31

| Topic | Article | Legal character |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Explanation principle | Art. 3(2) | Principle: explanation where **technically and reasonably possible** — not a GDPR individual appeal clock |
| High-impact explanation measures | Art. 34(1)2 | Operators of high-impact AI **establish measures** covering results, main criteria, and training-data overview |
| Art. 31 notices | Art. 31 | Separate from explanation measures |

## Operator split (Art. 31 only)

| Role | Article | Art. 31 task |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Development operator | Art. 2(7)(a), Art. 31 | Build prior-notice and labelling into the product |
| Use/service operator | Art. 2(7)(b), Art. 31 | Display notice/labels to persons provided with the service |
| Foreign operator | Art. 36, Art. 31 | Same paragraphs if Korea-facing; representative does not replace the notice duty |
| Public institution | Art. 30(4), Art. 31 | Same paragraphs when providing the product/service |
| User (person provided with the product) | Art. 2(8) | Rights-holder receiving notice — **not** an obligated Art. 31 column |
