# Art. 36 domestic representative

Foreign AI business operators with a Korean nexus may have to designate a **domestic representative**. Failure can attract an administrative fine up to **KRW 30 million** (Art. 43).

The user (Art. 2(8)) is not the representative and is not an obligated operator column.

## When Art. 36 applies

| Condition | Article / instrument | In scope if |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------|
| Foreign operator provides AI in or into Korea | Art. 36 | Korean nexus |
| Total revenue | Art. 36 + Enforcement Decree No. 36053 | **≥ KRW 1 trillion** |
| AI-related revenue | Art. 36 + decree | **≥ KRW 10 billion** |
| Average daily Korean users | Art. 36 + decree | **≥ 1 million** |
| Corrective-order fine | Art. 36 + decree | Operator was **fined for violating a corrective order** |

Designate a representative if there is a Korean nexus **and** **any** revenue/user/corrective-order limb is met.

## What to designate

| Item | Article | Record |
|------|---------|--------|
| Domestic representative identity | Art. 36 | Name, Korean address, contact |
| Mandate | Art. 36 | Authority to receive MSIT communications and act on Art. 31–36 matters |
| Operator type of the foreign principal | Art. 2(7), Art. 36 | Development and/or use-service |
| Threshold arithmetic | Art. 36 + decree | Which limb(s) are met (revenue, users, or corrective-order fine) |
| Public-institution extra duties | Art. 30(4), Art. 35(2) | Only if the foreign principal is (or sells to) a public body — representative does not erase those duties |

## What Art. 36 does not do

| Claim | Article | Rule |
|-------|---------|------|
| Replaces Art. 31 notices | Art. 31, Art. 36 | Notice duties remain with the operator |
| Replaces Art. 34 measures | Art. 34, Art. 36 | High-impact measures remain |
| EU authorised-representative copy | Art. 36 | Closest analogue only; Korea thresholds differ |
| Applies to Art. 2(8) users | Art. 2(8), Art. 36 | Users are persons provided with the product, not the foreign operator |

## Closest analogue

| Korea | Article | Closest analogue | Do not equate |
|-------|---------|------------------|---------------|
| Domestic representative | Art. 36 | EU AI Act authorised representative | Different triggers (KRW 1T / KRW 10B AI / 1M daily users / corrective-order fine) |
| Supplier / foreign operator control | Art. 36 | ISO/IEC 42001 **A.10** suppliers/customers | A.10 is a management-system control |
