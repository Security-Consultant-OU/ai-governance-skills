# GPAI, generative AI, synthetic content, and copyright — Senate-approved PL 2338/2023 (not enacted)

**Label:** based on Senate-approved PL 2338/2023; not enacted. This file restates **proposed** Senate chapters.

The Senate substitute **skips Art. 31** (Art. 30 then Art. 32).

---

## Arts. 19–20 — Synthetic content labelling (proposed)

| Article | Proposed rule |
|---------|----------------|
| Art. 19 caput | When the system generates **conteúdo sintético**, include an identifier to verify authenticity or provenance, modification, or transmission, considering state of the art and context of use, per regulation |
| Art. 19 §1 | Identifier does **not** replace other information/transparency duties |
| Art. 19 §2 | Autoridade competente, with **Cria**, to provide a software library, ideally an internationally recognised standard |
| Art. 19 §3 | Artistic, cultural, or entertainment works may be signalled in credits or metadata when there is no material risk of disseminating false information |
| Art. 4 XXI | Synthetic content = information (images, video, audio, text) significantly modified or generated by AI |
| Art. 20 | Public authorities with private sector, civil society, and R&D to promote capabilities to identify/label synthetic content and establish authenticity/provenance |

## Arts. 29–33 — GPAI and generative AI (proposed)

Definitions: **SIAPG** Art. 4 III; **IA generativa** Art. 4 IV; **risco sistêmico** Art. 4 XXX (adverse effects of GPAI/generative systems with significant impact on individual and social fundamental rights).

| Article | Who | Proposed duty |
|---------|-----|----------------|
| Art. 29 | Desenvolvedor of GPAI and generative systems | Documentation of development **plus** Art. 12-style **preliminary assessment** of expected risk levels, including **potential systemic risk**. Consider reasonably expected uses and Art. 14 high-risk criteria (Seção III Capítulo III) |
| Art. 30 caput | Desenvolvedor of GPAI/generative systems **with systemic risk**, before commercial availability / market introduction | I describe the general-purpose model; II document tests/analyses of reasonably foreseeable risks; III document remaining unmitigable risks; IV data collected/processed lawfully, with data governance, LGPD if personal data; V **publish a summary of training datasets**, per regulation; VI energy/resource efficiency; VII intelligible technical documentation and instructions for desenvolvedores, distribuidores, and aplicadores |
| Art. 30 §1 | Same | Duties apply whether the system is standalone, embedded, open-source, service, or other distribution |
| Art. 30 §2 | Same | Codes of practice may demonstrate conformity |
| Art. 32 | GPAI/generative desenvolvedores offering the system as a resource for third-party services (**API** or other integration) | Cooperate, in proportion to participation, while the service is provided, so risks can be mitigated and Chapter II rights met |
| Art. 33 | Autoridade competente + SIA | May simplify or waive this Seção by risk and state of the art; Capítulo VI codes of conduct apply; competent authority approves GPAI codes |

There is **no Art. 31** in the Senate substitute.

## Arts. 62–64 — Training-data copyright (proposed)

Seção IV do Capítulo X. Vacatio: most of this Seção at **180 days**; **Art. 62 immediate** on publication (Art. 80 §1 III).

| Article | Proposed rule |
|---------|----------------|
| Art. 62 | Desenvolvedor that uses copyright- and neighbouring-rights-protected content must **inform**, via a **summary on an easily accessible website**, which protected contents were used in development, trade secrets reserved, per specific regulation |
| Art. 62 parágrafo único | “Development” includes mining, training, retraining, testing, validation, and application |
| Art. 63 | Automated TDM of protected content for R&D of AI systems by scientific, research, and educational organisations, museums, public archives, and libraries is **not** a copyright infringement if: I lawful access; II **non-commercial**; III only as needed, without prejudicing rightholders’ economic interests or competing with normal exploitation |
| Art. 63 limits | Secure storage only as needed; no display/dissemination of protected works used; does not apply to for-profit-controlled affiliates that supply AI systems; TDM to combat copyright crime/civil wrongs may use the exception |
| Art. 64 | Rightholder may **prohibit** use of their contents in AI development in hypotheses **not** covered by Art. 63 |
| Art. 64 parágrafo único | Opt-out **after** training does not excuse liability for moral and material damages |
| Art. 65 | Remuneration duty for mining/training/development use, with collective or direct negotiation (detail in the bill; reciprocity rules) |

EU GPAI documentation **does not** satisfy Art. 62’s Portuguese-facing copyright **summary** or Art. 64 opt-out.
