# Obligations by role — Senate-approved PL 2338/2023 (not enacted)

**Label:** based on Senate-approved PL 2338/2023; not enacted.

Legal roles are **desenvolvedor, distribuidor, aplicador** (Art. 4 V–VII). **Agentes de IA** = those three (Art. 4 VIII).

English “deployer” is **not** a legal category. Use it only as an informal synonym for **aplicador**. Do not use EU **operator** as a Brazilian legal role.

---

## Art. 4 role definitions

| Role | Art. 4 | Definition (abridged) |
|------|--------|------------------------|
| Desenvolvedor | V | Natural or legal person (public or private) that develops an AI system, directly or by order, to place it on the market or apply it in a service it supplies, under its own name or brand, for a fee or free |
| Distribuidor | VI | Makes an AI system available and distributes it so a **third party** applies it, for a fee or free |
| Aplicador | VII | Employs or uses an AI system in its own name or benefit, including configuring, maintaining, or supporting with data for operation and monitoring |

Art. 18 §5: aplicador or distribuidor that makes a **substantial modification** or changes **purpose** is treated as **desenvolvedor**.

## Role determination

| Scenario | Desenvolvedor | Distribuidor | Aplicador |
|----------|---------------|--------------|-----------|
| Builds and uses own system in Brazil | Yes | No (unless also distributing to third parties) | Yes |
| Builds system sold to others | Yes | No | No |
| Distributes a third-party system without applying it | No | Yes | No |
| Uses a third-party system in own operations | No (unless Art. 18 §5) | No | Yes |
| Fine-tunes / substantially modifies a third-party system | Yes (Art. 18 §5) | Possibly | Yes |
| Mere hosting / data transport | Excluded Art. 1 §1 IV | Excluded if only infrastructure | Excluded if only infrastructure |

An entity may hold **multiple** roles. Substantial modification (Art. 18 §5) is the main reclassification trigger.

| Topic | Split |
