# Sanctions, liability, and vacatio — Senate-approved PL 2338/2023 (not enacted)

**Label:** based on Senate-approved PL 2338/2023; not enacted. This file restates **proposed** Senate text. Nothing here is enforceable as an AI statute today.

---

## Arts. 35–36 — Civil liability (proposed)

| Article | Proposed rule |
|---------|----------------|
| Art. 35 | Damages from AI in **consumer** relations remain under the **CDC** (Lei 8.078/1990) and related legislation, without prejudice to this bill |
| Art. 36 | Damages from AI explored, employed, or used by agentes de IA remain under the **Civil Code** (Lei 10.406/2002) and special legislation |
| Art. 36 parágrafo único | Concrete regime considers: I autonomy and risk degree of the system; II nature of the agents and any special liability regime |
| Art. 37 | Judge **inverts the burden of proof** when the victim is hyposufficient or when the system’s operation would make proof excessively onerous |
| Art. 38 | Sandbox participants remain liable for third-party harm from sandbox experimentation |
| Art. 39 | Specific liability hypotheses in other statutes remain in force |

This is **not** a new standalone AI tort code. It points to CDC / Civil Code.

---

## Art. 50 — Administrative sanctions (proposed)

Applicable to **agentes de IA** for infractions of the bill. Art. 80: Art. 50 itself does **not** enter into force on publication (Capítulo IX in force on publication **except** Art. 50).

| Inciso | Sanction |
|--------|----------|
| I | **Advertência** (warning) |
| II | **Multa simples**, capped at **R$ 50.000.000,00** per infraction; for private legal persons, up to **2% of gross turnover** of the person, group, or conglomerate **in Brazil** in the last financial year, excluding taxes |
| III | Publicisation of the infraction after it is duly ascertained and confirmed |
| IV | Prohibition or restriction on participating in the **sandbox** for up to **5 years** |
| V | Partial or total, temporary or definitive **suspension** of development, supply, or operation of the AI system |
| VI | Prohibition on processing specified databases |

Art. 50 §1: after administrative due process; graduated; isolated or cumulative. Criteria include gravity, good faith, advantage, economic condition, recidivism, damage, cooperation, internal risk-minimisation (including AIA and ethics code), good-practice policy, prompt corrective measures, proportionality, and cumulation with other final administrative sanctions for the same act.

Turnover is **Brazilian** turnover — not global EU-style turnover.

## Art. 80 — Vacatio (proposed; only if enacted and published)

| When | What |
|------|------|
| **730 days** after official publication | General entry into force (caput) |
| **180 days** after publication | Art. **13** (prohibited practices); Seção V Capítulo IV (**GPAI/generative**, Arts. 29–33); Seção IV Capítulo X (**copyright**, Arts. 62–66) **except Art. 62** |
| **Immediate** (on publication) | **Art. 62** (training-data copyright summary) |
| **On publication** | Capítulo IX (SIA / supervision) **except Art. 50**; Capítulo X Seção III (environmental fostering); Capítulo X Seção V (MEI/PME/startup criteria) |

Do **not** invent other clocks (no 72-hour incident deadline; no 5-year log retention).

## SIA start-up (proposed)

| Article | Note |
|---------|------|
| Art. 45 | Executive **may** establish SIA; Senate text names ANPD as coordinator with sectoral authorities, Cria, Cecia. Chamber may change this |
| Art. 74 | Within **2 years**, Executive to provide ANPD resources (including administrative restructuring); define sectoral-authority list; define Cria composition; define Cecia composition and competences |

Until SIA exists, treat coordinator, sectoral lists (Art. 16), and Art. 42 prazos as **unset**.
